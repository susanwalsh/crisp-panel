The Crisp Panel.

Eleven crisps. Twenty-three judges. Three medals each.

Taste as many as you like, then award gold, silver and bronze. Gold earns a crisp 3 points, silver 2 and bronze 1. The panel’s decision is final. The crumbs are inevitable.

Checking the register…

Check before you taste

Most of the line-up is labelled vegan. Pipers Burrow Hill Cider Vinegar & Sea Salt contains milk, so it isn’t suitable for vegans. Tyrrells Lentil Sweet Chilli & Red Pepper is labelled vegetarian but not vegan.

Some packets say they may contain soya, gluten, tree nuts or peanuts. Check the packet or tap Ingredients on any crisp before you taste.

2 Get your taste on

Prompts to think about. You don’t need to answer them.

  • First bite and flavour
  • Crunch and texture
  • Crisp dust and quiet-meeting potential
  • Does the packet deliver what it promises?
  • Lick-your-fingers factor
  • Can you stop at just one?
  • Would you buy it again?

3 Award your medals

Choose a different crisp for 1st, 2nd and 3rd. Tap a medal again to take it back. You don’t have to taste every crisp, but it is strongly recommended.
